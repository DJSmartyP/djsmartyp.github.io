# DJ Smarty – One and Done 2.0
An unofficial, mobile-friendly Spellinator Level 4 badge assistant.

Live site: https://djsmartyp.github.io/spellinator-assist/

## Fixed rounds
Round 1: 4 distinct letters. Round 2: 5 distinct letters. Round 3: 6 distinct letters.
No letter may be repeated within a word or reused in later rounds.

## Enter available letters
Enter the current round's visible letters, then tap a proposed word *after the game has accepted it*. The helper only suggests words using those letters that meet the required word length and exclude previously played letters. Tapping the word records it and opens the next round.

## Enter already played word
If you have already completed a round, choose **Enter played word** and type the actual word. The helper validates the correct length, distinct letters, and no overlap with earlier rounds. Tap **Record word & next round** to save and immediately advance. You don't need to enter the available letters.

Switch freely between both modes. Back lets you correct a prior word; changing it clears later choices. Progress is remembered on your device.

## Limits
The dictionary is not Activate's official word list. Only record words accepted by the physical game. This assistant does not award the badge.
