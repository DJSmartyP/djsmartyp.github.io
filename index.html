Approved foods & planning notes

The source is JAG’s “Approved food Lists.pdf”, supplied 22 October 2026. It provides one shared approved ingredient list and food ideas. The planner uses a subset of those ingredients for breakfast and after-school sessions.

Portions and pack yields are estimates, not company standards. The source limits condiments to 10g across the school day; this planner caps them at 10g per child per day, or 5g per session for a combined club. Staff must account for condiments served elsewhere in the day.

Exclusions work on ingredient names, not on allergens in packaged products. Staff must check product labels, dietary records, use-by dates and chilled storage. This tool is a planning aid, not a nutrition or allergen certification system.

The full-period forecast repeats this week’s attendance, full-pack purchases, prices and fees for the chosen number of weeks. It does not carry leftovers into later weeks. No planning data is sent to a server.
